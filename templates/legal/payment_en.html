This Payment Policy explains how you pay Bitpaw Technology LLC for BitPaw Software, and how payments your own customers make through the Service work. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Currencies and prices

  • We bill in US dollars (USD) or Australian dollars (AUD). You choose the currency when you sign up.
  • Each plan has a one-time setup fee and a monthly fee, shown on our pricing page and confirmed on your invoice.
  • US: state and local sales tax is added where it applies. Australia: GST is added where it applies, and we issue tax invoices.

2. How billing works

  1. You send a signup request. Nothing is charged at this step.
  2. We confirm your setup with you, then email an invoice for the setup fee and the first month.
  3. You pay the invoice by card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover in the US; Visa, Mastercard, American Express in Australia) or Apple Pay/Google Pay, through Square's secure payment page.
  4. After that, we invoice monthly in advance on the same day of the month. You can ask us to save your card for automatic monthly payment.

We never see or store your full card number; Square handles it under PCI DSS.

3. Failed or late payments

If a payment fails we email you and try again over the next 7 days. If an invoice is still unpaid 14 days after our notice, we may pause the Service until it is paid. Your data is kept safe while the Service is paused.

4. Changing plans

Upgrades take effect straight away; we invoice the difference for the rest of the month. Downgrades take effect from your next billing date.

5. Cancellations and refunds

  • Cancel any time; the Service stays on until the end of the month you have paid for.
  • We do not refund part-months. The setup fee is not refundable once we have started setup work.
  • If we charge you by mistake, we refund the full amount to the original card, usually within 5–10 business days.
  • Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law and other consumer protection laws are not affected.

6. Disputes

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank — most billing questions are solved in one email. If a chargeback is opened, we may pause the account while it is resolved.

7. Payments from your customers

When your customers pay you through BitPaw (for example with a Square Terminal or a deposit link), the money goes directly to your own payment account. Processing fees are charged by your payment provider under your agreement with them; BitPaw does not add fees to those payments. Refunds to your customers are made from your payment account, and BitPaw records them in your reports.
