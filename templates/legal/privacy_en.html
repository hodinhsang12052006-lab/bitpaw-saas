Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bitpaw Technology LLC ("BitPaw", "we") handles personal information in BitPaw Software — our website, web app, and Windows, Android and iPhone/iPad apps (the "Service"). It covers the US (including California) and Australia (including the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988).

1. Two roles we play

  • For business owners and visitors to our website, we decide how your information is used. This policy applies directly to you.
  • For the information a business puts into the Service — its customers, appointments, sales and staff — we process it on behalf of that business, as its service provider. That business decides what is collected and why. If you are a customer or employee of a business that uses BitPaw, please contact that business first; we will help them respond.

2. What we collect

  • Account and signup details: name, business name, email, phone, country, industry, plan and billing records. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Sign in with Google or Facebook (optional): if you choose it, we receive only your name, email address, whether that email is verified, your profile picture link and an account identifier from Google or Facebook. We use them only to create and sign in to your BitPaw account. We never receive your Google or Facebook password, never post on your behalf, and do not read your contacts, friends, emails or files. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Customer records a business keeps: names, phone numbers, emails, appointment and purchase history, notes, loyalty points, and messages sent through the booking page or chat.
  • Staff records a business keeps: name, employee code, role, pay rates, clock-in and clock-out times, and — only if the business turns them on — GPS location at clock-in, a photo at clock-in, and a face template for face check-in.
  • Payments: transaction amounts and references. Card payments are processed by Square; we never receive or store full card numbers.
  • Messages: SMS and email sent through the Service, delivery status and opt-outs.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser and device type, pages used, and error logs, used to keep the Service secure and working.

3. Face check-in (biometric information)

Face check-in is off unless a business turns it on and each employee agrees. When it is on, the staff app turns a photo into a numeric face template on the employee's own device and compares it with that employee's saved template. We store only the template and the match result, not a library of face photos, and use it only to confirm who is clocking in. Templates are deleted when the employee is removed, when the business turns the feature off, or within 30 days after the business cancels, whichever comes first. Employees who do not agree can clock in another way.

4. How we use information

  • To run the Service: bookings, checkout, pay and commission, reminders, reports and support.
  • To send messages a business asks us to send (reminders, review requests, missed-call replies, marketing it is allowed to send).
  • To power AI features a business uses, such as chat assistants and content writing.
  • To secure the Service, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix problems.
  • To bill business customers and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use a business's customer or staff data for our own marketing.

5. Who we share it with

  • Service providers that help run the Service: MongoDB Atlas (database and file storage), Vercel (hosting), Square (payments), Twilio (SMS and calls), Google (email delivery, maps and review links) and DeepSeek (AI text generation). They may only use the information to provide their service to us.
  • The business you deal with, for information it collects through the Service.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, and professional advisers bound by confidentiality.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is sold or merged, under the same protections.

6. Information sent overseas

Our systems are hosted in the United States. If you are in Australia, your information will be stored and processed in the United States. When an AI feature is used, the text needed to answer the request (for example a chat message or a content prompt) is sent to DeepSeek, which may process it in China. We send only what the feature needs, never passwords or card details, and businesses can switch AI features off. We take reasonable steps so that overseas recipients handle personal information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Each business's data is kept separate and checked on every request, passwords are hashed, access to production systems is restricted, and we test the Service for security issues before releases. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected businesses and, where required, individuals and regulators (including under Australia's Notifiable Data Breaches scheme and US state breach laws).

8. How long we keep it

We keep Customer Data while the business account is active and for 30 days after cancellation so it can be exported, then delete it. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires. Opt-out records are kept so we keep honouring them.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Everyone: you can ask to access or correct your information, or ask us to delete it. Reply STOP to any SMS to stop texts; use the unsubscribe link in emails.
  • California residents: you have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, to limit use of sensitive personal information, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA/CPRA.
  • Australia: you can access and correct your personal information under the Australian Privacy Principles. If you are unhappy with how we handle a complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
  • Business owners can delete their account themselves at Delete Account.
  • Google or Facebook sign-in: you can disconnect BitPaw at any time in your Google Account (Security → Third-party access) or Facebook (Settings → Apps and Websites). To delete the data we hold, use Delete Account or email us (section 12) — we delete it within 30 days.

We respond to requests within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first, and if the information belongs to a business that uses BitPaw, we will pass your request to that business.

10. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in, remember your language and currency, and protect forms. We do not use advertising cookies.

11. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in Australia). We do not knowingly collect their information.
