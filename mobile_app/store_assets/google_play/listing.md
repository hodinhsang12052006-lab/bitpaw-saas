## en-US

Short description (80):
POS, online booking, staff attendance and payroll for salons and small business

Full description:
BitPaw Software is the business app for owners and staff who already use BitPaw on the web. Sign in with your BitPaw account to run your shop from your phone.

FOR OWNERS AND MANAGERS
• Point of sale: open tickets, add services or products, discounts, refunds and receipts
• Online booking: see and manage appointments and reservations from your booking page
• Customers: customer list, visit history and notes
• Staff: attendance with photo and GPS check-in, time sheets, commission and payroll
• Reports: sales and staff performance in your own currency
• Messaging inbox: replies to customers from your connected channels in one place

FOR STAFF
• Clock in and out from your phone
• See your schedule, jobs and earnings
• Request leave and reimbursements

BUILT FOR MANY KINDS OF BUSINESS
Nail salons, spas, restaurants and cafés, karaoke and billiards, hotels, retail stores, technical and repair services, workshops and offices — each with screens made for how that business works.

GOOD TO KNOW
• You need a BitPaw account. Your shop owner can add you as staff.
• Camera and location are used only when you check in for a shift or attach a photo, and only after you allow them.
• Your data stays in your BitPaw account. You can request account deletion at https://www.bitpawsoftware.com/account/delete

Questions? Email support@bitpawsoftware.com
